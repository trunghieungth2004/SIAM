## About the Event

**KICK-OFF AWS FIRST CLOUD JOURNEY WORKFORCE OJT FALL 2025**

The university is honored to congratulate students who have successfully passed the AWS First Cloud Journey – Training on the Job (OJT FALL 2025) program. This is an important milestone, marking the beginning of a learning journey and practical experience with Amazon Web Services (AWS), while preparing a solid foundation for careers in Cloud Computing, AI, and DevOps.

### About AWS First Cloud Journey Workforce

- Launched in 2021, the program has accompanied over 2,000 students nationwide.
- More than 150 students have received in-depth training and are currently working at leading technology companies in Vietnam and internationally.

**Main objectives:**

- Build a high-quality generation of AWS Builders for Vietnam.
- Equip practical skills in Cloud, DevOps, AI/ML, Security, Data & Analytics.
- Connect students with the AWS Study Group community of 47,000+ members and AWS partner businesses.

The program is not just about technology training, but also an important bridge between knowledge – technology – career, helping students confidently integrate into the modern technology world and international integration.
